Each converter stands on its own. The RPM card at the bottom is a separate calculator.

Surface speed SFM = SMM × 3.28084

⇄

Feed per minute IPM = mm/min ÷ 25.4

⇄

Feed per revolution IPR = mm/rev ÷ 25.4

⇄

Size 1 in = 25.4 mm

⇄

Surface roughness 1 µm = 39.3701 µin

⇄

Torque 1 N·m = 0.737562 ft·lb = 8.85075 in·lb

Spindle speed & feed RPM = (SMM × 1000) / (π × D)

Mix units however you like. Feed/min = Feed/rev × RPM. Use the cutter diameter for milling and drilling, or the part diameter for turning.
